# Notion-Inspired Design System Mandates

All development in this project must strictly adhere to the following design system.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
- **Canvas:** Pure white (`#ffffff`).
- **Text:** Near-black (`rgba(0,0,0,0.95)`).
- **Tone:** Warm neutrals (yellow-brown undertones), not cold grays.
- **Borders:** "Whisper" weight: `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`.
- **Shadows:** Multi-layer stacks with cumulative opacity ≤ 0.05.
- **Font:** `NotionInter` (or `Inter`) with specific letter-spacing compression at larger sizes.

## 2. Core Palette
- **Notion Blue (`#0075de`):** Primary CTA, links, and interactive accents.
- **Warm White (`#f6f5f4`):** Background tints and section alternation.
- **Warm Dark (`#31302e`):** Dark surfaces.
- **Warm Gray 500 (`#615d59`):** Secondary text.
- **Warm Gray 300 (`#a39e98`):** Muted/Placeholder text.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Display Hero (64px):** Weight 700, Line-height 1.00, Letter-spacing -2.125px.
- **Section Heading (48px):** Weight 700, Line-height 1.00, Letter-spacing -1.5px.
- **Card Title (22px):** Weight 700, Line-height 1.27, Letter-spacing -0.25px.
- **Body (16px):** Weight 400, Line-height 1.50, Letter-spacing normal.
- **Nav/Button (15px):** Weight 600, Line-height 1.33.
- **Badge (12px):** Weight 600, Letter-spacing 0.125px (positive tracking).

## 4. Component Standards
- **Buttons:** 4px radius. Primary: Notion Blue. Secondary: `rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`.
- **Cards:** 12px radius, whisper border, multi-layer shadow.
- **Pill Badges:** 9999px radius, tinted blue background (`#f2f9ff`).
- **Inputs:** 4px radius, 1px solid `#dddddd`.

## 5. Layout & Spacing
- **Base Unit:** 8px (organic scale allowed).
- **Max Width:** 1200px.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 64-120px between major sections.
- **Alternation:** Switch between white and `#f6f5f4` for section backgrounds.
